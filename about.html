Our Story

Books belong in more hands, not on shelves.

BookBridge started as a with a simple idea : most of us have books gathering dust that someone nearby would love to read. We built a place to share, borrow, and exchange them no clutter, no cost, just readers helping readers.

What We Believe

Sustainability

Every shared book is one less printed copy needed.

Community

Reading is better when it connects you to someone nearby.

Accessibility

Great books shouldn't be locked behind a price tag.
